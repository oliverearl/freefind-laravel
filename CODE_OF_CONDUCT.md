# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want this project to be a respectful, professional place for people with different backgrounds and experience levels. Participants must:

- communicate constructively and assume good faith;
- respect differing technical views and lived experiences;
- focus criticism on ideas, code, and documentation rather than people;
- avoid harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualised language, and deliberate disruption; and
- respect privacy, particularly in security reports and examples that could expose accounts, queries, or indexed content.

## Scope

This standard applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, releases, and other project spaces, as well as project-related conduct in public spaces when it has a material effect on the community.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to [oliver@oliverearl.co.uk](mailto:oliver@oliverearl.co.uk). Include only the information needed to investigate; reports will be handled as discreetly as reasonably possible.

The maintainer may clarify expectations, request a correction or apology, remove content, limit participation, or take another proportionate action to protect the community. Retaliation against a person who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.
